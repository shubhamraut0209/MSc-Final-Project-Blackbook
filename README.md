# ✈️ Statistical Analysis of Airline Service Quality & Customer Loyalty

### MSc Statistics — Final Year Research Project

A comprehensive statistical research project investigating **service quality, customer loyalty, passenger emotions, and demographic factors** across Indian domestic airlines.

This project was completed as part of my **MSc in Statistics at K.C. College, HSNC University** and involved primary data collection, statistical modeling, multivariate analysis, machine learning techniques, and data visualization.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The airline industry is highly dependent on customer experience and service quality.

This study investigates how different dimensions of airline service quality influence:

- Customer loyalty
- Passenger perceptions
- Emotional responses
- Overall service experience

The project combines **statistical analysis, machine learning, and data visualization** to identify meaningful relationships within the survey data.

---

## 🎯 Research Objectives

- Identify demographic factors associated with customer loyalty.
- Analyze passenger perceptions of airline service quality.
- Study relationships between service quality dimensions and customer loyalty.
- Analyze passenger emotional responses to service experiences.
- Identify meaningful customer segments.
- Develop statistical and predictive models to understand customer behavior.
- Derive actionable insights for improving airline service quality.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The study was based on **primary survey data collected through structured questionnaires** from passengers of Indian domestic airlines.

The dataset contains information relating to:

- Passenger demographics
- Service quality perceptions
- Customer loyalty
- Passenger emotions
- Airline experience
